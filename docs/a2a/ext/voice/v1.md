# A2A extension: voice/v1

- **URI:** `https://madarauchiha-314.github.io/rusty-harness/a2a/ext/voice/v1`
- **Schema:** [`v1.schema.json`](./v1.schema.json) (JSON Schema, generated from the Rust
  types and contract-tested against `specs/a2a-ext/voice.v1.schema.json`)
- **Rides on:** the metadata of an audio `Part`

## What it carries

An audio chunk's place in a speech session: its sequence number, the loop attempt that produced it,
the encoding (`pcm16@24k` or `opus`), whether it is the final chunk, and the session id.

## Activation

Send `A2A-Extensions: https://madarauchiha-314.github.io/rusty-harness/a2a/ext/voice/v1`
(several URIs comma-separated) on a request; the server applies the extensions it
advertises and echoes them in the response's `A2A-Extensions`. The metadata key is the
URI itself. Back to [all extensions](../).
